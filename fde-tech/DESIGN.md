# FDE Tech design

Reuse JR Craft semantic `--jc-*` tokens from the brand repository. Warm canvas, white rounded cards, sky surfaces for orientation, semantic focus rings, two-column desktop week cards and one-column mobile learning cards. The sales site retains its canonical components. Curriculum tables scroll within their own container; page content must not overflow. All pages use noindex/nofollow; no GA, provider keys, enrolment or model calls. This is a reading and engineering-practice course interface, not a slide deck.
