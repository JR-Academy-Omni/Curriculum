本周工程任务

TASK 16

Bounded Agent Execution

Define steps, tools, time and cost limits, and verify when an agent continues, pauses or stops.

Deliverable

Agent state machine and execution-boundary checks.

TASK 19

Human Handover and Resume

Preserve state for human decisions, then resume from the correct step without repeating completed actions.

Deliverable

Handover record, checkpoints and resume implementation.

TASK 20

Recovery after Partial Failure

Reconcile partially completed actions before choosing compensation, retry or human intervention.

Deliverable

Reconciliation flow, compensation strategy and recovery checks.

TASK 24

Latency and Unit Economics

Compare latency, model spend and human review across workloads, estimating cost per accepted outcome.

Deliverable

Latency measurements, unit-cost analysis and optimisation trade-offs.

TASK 25

Observability and Incident Diagnosis

Trace tools, errors, costs and decisions across a request, retaining useful evidence while controlling sensitive log content.

Deliverable

Traces, alert conditions and incident diagnosis records.
