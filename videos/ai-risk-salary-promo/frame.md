## Frame language

Executive technology editorial: deep navy field, ivory type, lime for money and action,
purple for governance. Large typography carries the story; diagrams provide evidence.
Use glassy dark panels sparingly, soft glows, fine grid lines and visible motion trails.

## Motion

Continuous shared-element handoffs. Numbers become cards, cards become nodes, nodes become
risk signals, signals become the Three Lines of Defence, then fold into a learning path and
Case Pack. No slide-to-slide fades. Favor overshoot, directional wipes, tracked line draws,
counter motion and velocity matching.

## Guardrails
