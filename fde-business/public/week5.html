Knowledge check

A customer changes the preferred time after a reply draft is approved. What should happen?

  • Send the old reply.
  • Reuse the old approval for all revisions.
  • Update the draft and request a new approval.
Answer and reasoning

Update the draft and request a new approval.

Approval applies to the content and revision that was reviewed.

Reference reasoning

A repeated customer enquiry updates the existing record. A changed preferred time invalidates the old draft approval. A changed employee role invalidates the prior access decision; IT still owns provisioning.
