Knowledge check

Nine outputs are correct and one contains an unauthorized commitment. Can the average score justify release?

  • Yes.
  • No; the control failure needs correction and rechecking.
  • Only if the file opens.
Answer and reasoning

No; the control failure needs correction and rechecking.

Critical failures cannot be averaged away.

Reference reasoning

A correct duplicate flag is a useful result, not an error to conceal. An unresolved tax status stays with finance. Onboarding is incomplete until each required owner supplies the right evidence.

Knowledge check

A budget forecasts twenty hours of saved effort each month. Which statement is supported?

  • Twenty hours of capacity is estimated under the stated assumptions.
  • Twenty hours is guaranteed.
  • The company has already received a cash saving.
Answer and reasoning

Twenty hours of capacity is estimated under the stated assumptions.

A forecast is not an observed result or cash saving.

Reference reasoning

For the invoice case, estimate 20 hours of monthly capacity at 80% coverage and five minutes saved per accepted invoice. State assumptions. Reduced reviewer availability changes turnaround and queue design, not the need for approval.
