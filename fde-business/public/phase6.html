Decision to obtain

Accept the handover or request specific corrections.

Knowledge check

A colleague cannot handle an exception without the author. What should happen before handover acceptance?

  • Accept because the author can do it.
  • Remove the exception.
  • Improve the instructions and have the receiver repeat the check.
Answer and reasoning

Improve the instructions and have the receiver repeat the check.

Handover tests whether another person can operate the workflow with the materials.

Reference reasoning

Handover is accepted when the receiver can trace an invoice field, explain a duplicate decision, revalidate a changed customer draft and identify the HR/IT owner of an onboarding blocker. An attractive presentation alone is insufficient.
