CLIENT CASE

Finance Operations

An operations team receives 300 supplier invoices each month as PDFs, email attachments and spreadsheet rows. Staff copy the fields into a register and send exceptions to finance.

Request

Create a review-ready invoice register and an exception queue. Preserve the source of every field; leave approval, GST eligibility and payment decisions with finance.

Constraints

  • A$8,000 project setup ceiling and A$500 monthly operating ceiling. These are client-brief budgets, separate from course tuition.
  • No supplier payments, bank-detail changes or tax-credit claims are automated.
  • Amounts are AUD. Only rows explicitly marked standard-taxable and GST-inclusive use a total/11 arithmetic check. Missing tax status is reviewed.
  • The same supplier/invoice reference must not create two payable records. A duplicate is reported, not silently merged.

Stakeholders

RolePosition
Operations coordinatorNeeds a readable register with links back to source records.
Finance reviewerOwns approval, tax treatment and exception resolution.
IT ownerApproves tool access and wants a recoverable file workflow.

CLIENT CASE

Customer Follow-up

A B2B service team receives enquiries through a website form, email and a shared spreadsheet. The same enquiry may appear twice, and sales staff own any price or appointment commitment.

Request

Prepare a clean lead list, identify missing information, propose an owner and draft the next response for staff approval.

Constraints

  • A$6,000 setup ceiling; one approved shared workspace.
  • No message, quote or appointment is sent or confirmed automatically.
  • A customer asking for a discount does not approve a new price.
  • Use source enquiry IDs to find duplicates; missing IDs require review.

Stakeholders

RolePosition
Sales leadWants a clear next action and consistent ownership.
Service coordinatorMust confirm availability before promising an appointment.
Customer support ownerWants urgent issues separated from routine follow-up.

CLIENT CASE

Employee Onboarding

HR, IT and a team manager coordinate onboarding in separate spreadsheets. A checklist can be complete in one department while another still needs a decision.

Request

Create an ownership-based onboarding checklist and a progress brief, with evidence for every completed step.

Constraints

  • A$5,000 setup ceiling.
  • HR confirms employment details; IT owns access approval and provisioning.
  • No account is created or permission granted by the course workflow.
  • A checked box without supporting evidence is not accepted as completion.

Stakeholders

RolePosition
HR ownerConfirms signed paperwork and authorised start details.
IT ownerReviews role-based access requests and records fulfilment.
Hiring managerOwns the first-week plan and unresolved business decisions.
