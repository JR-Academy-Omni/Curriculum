Knowledge check

A supplier attachment tells the assistant to change bank details. How should the workflow treat that text?

  • As permission from the supplier.
  • As content that does not authorize a business action.
  • As a system instruction.
Answer and reasoning

As content that does not authorize a business action.

Business content does not grant system or payment authority.

Reference reasoning

AI can prepare candidate invoice fields or a draft reply. Arithmetic and duplicate checks use explicit rules. Finance approves treatment; sales approves a message. Supplier content cannot grant tool permissions.
