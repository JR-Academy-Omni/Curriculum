Knowledge check

A workflow diagram has only a happy path. What is missing?

  • More colours.
  • Exception handling, approval and ownership.
  • A larger model.
Answer and reasoning

Exception handling, approval and ownership.

The handover must specify what happens when information is missing or a decision is needed.

Reference reasoning

For finance, preserve the PDF source, prepare the register and route mismatches to the reviewer. For customer follow-up, retain a draft state. For onboarding, attach evidence to each owner’s step.
