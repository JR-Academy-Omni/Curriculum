## 1. Positioning and message architecture

### Positioning

For small teams that manage their own content and customer acquisition: finish the brand visuals, templates, poster and basic merchandise, create social content and short video, then use AI to manage projects, company tasks, meeting actions and reviews.

### Product name and campaign line

- **Product name:** AI Content Marketing for Small Business
- **Lead campaign line:** Your one-person AI marketing team
- **Support line:** Automated content production × Brand design × AI project and business management
- **Core explanation:** AI handles production and automation; Agile handles priority, ownership, delivery and improvement.

### Six campaign directions

| Direction | Headline | Best use | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-person team | **Your one-person AI marketing team** | Awareness | Content, design, video and Company Task Board shown as one system |
| Automation | **Make your content workflow run repeatedly** | Education and demo | End-to-end Business Brief to platform asset workflow |
| Outcomes | **Finish the work before you finish the course** | Conversion | Thirteen one-hour units, eight outcome modules and the direct deliverables |
| Social media | **Plan 30 days of content without starting from zero every morning** | Consideration | Real 30-day calendar and 12 finished examples |
| Brand consistency | **Make every AI-generated asset look like the same brand** | Visual channels | Brand Kit, master templates and cross-format comparison |
| AI business management | **AI creates the work and helps run the company** | Deep explanation and Q&A | Project brief, Company Task Board, meeting actions, weekly report and next-cycle decisions |

### Claims policy

- Promise inspectable files, workflows and review practices only.
- Do not promise leads, revenue, hours saved, ROI or platform growth without measured course evidence.
- Do not use “fully automated acquisition”, “one-click viral growth”, “guaranteed results” or “no human review”.
- Label every example as a real business asset, classroom sample or demonstration dataset.

## 2. Funnel and measurement

```text
Useful teaching content or an outcome demo
  → Course landing page
  → Detailed outcomes and audience fit
  → Registration or enquiry CTA
  → Human fit check
  → Payment and confirmation
  → Pre-course Business & Brand Intake
```

Track reach, attributed visits, outline views, CTA clicks, valid leads, qualified leads, paid enrolments, attendance and completion as separate states. Every current value is `unavailable`; targets and conversion assumptions must wait for confirmed business inputs and historical evidence.

Use `utm_campaign=ai-marketing-agile-business-{intake}` only after the intake identifier is confirmed. Use the real platform for `utm_source` and one of `organic-social`, `email`, `webinar`, `community` or `referral` for `utm_medium`.

## 3. Channel plan

### P0 foundations

| Asset / channel | Job | Completion gate |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Receive all campaign traffic | Mobile and desktop QA; CTA, form, payment and confirmation tested |
| Real outcome demo | Prove the programme is practical | One authorised Business Brief → Content → Design → Sprint example |
| Persona evidence | Determine channels and language | English persona evidence coverage at or above 80% |
| Tracking | Connect content to enrolment | UTM, analytics and CRM source fields agree |

### Conditional channels

- **English webinar:** demonstrate one real campaign slice, then run a 24-hour, 72-hour and seven-day human follow-up process.
- **YouTube:** publish a three-to-eight-minute workflow demo only when video, narration, licensed music, captions, thumbnail, playlist and publishing fields pass review.
- **SEO:** build specific long-tail pages around small-business AI content workflows, brand consistency and agile marketing using real examples.
- **Google Business Profile:** use only for a confirmed local or online event with date, location, official link and local relevance.
- **Partner and community referrals:** activate only after the audience source and permission model are confirmed.

### Not active now

- LinkedIn Company Page: current account policy permits English AI Engineering architecture graphics, not course promotion.
- Instagram daily slot: current account positioning is English system-design content, so this course cannot displace it or masquerade as an educational daily post.
- TikTok: requires its independent English-native pipeline and media acceptance package.
- Google Ads and paid social: `manual_only`; no budget approval, conversion baseline or verified registration flow exists.
- Bulk private messaging: no authorised list or frequency policy; AI must not impersonate staff.

## 6. First nine content briefs

1. Turn one product into 30 days of social content.
2. Why one caption cannot simply be copied to every platform.
3. The most common brand-consistency failures in AI-generated design.
4. Extend one Brand Kit into posters, menus and merchandise.
5. The complete file set behind one short video.
6. Why “generated” is not the same as “ready to publish”.
7. How a small team uses a backlog to choose this week's work.
8. What belongs in the Definition of Done for a social content batch.
9. How to decide what to Stop, Change, Continue or Scale after publishing.

Before publication, every brief must be connected to validated persona evidence, a SOURCE, the correct account profile, an independent platform variant, media QA, human approval and duplicate checking.
