## 5. Three release waves

No dates are invented here. Once an intake is confirmed, map these waves to a real calendar and the Brisbane timezone.

### Wave A · Make the problem recognisable

Order: C01 → C02 → C04 → C05

- Goal: show that the constraint is not a lack of AI tools; it is weak inputs, missing brand rules and no repeatable system.
- CTA: save the framework, name the current blocker or view the free structure.
- Exclude prices, countdowns, capacity claims and inflated outcomes.

### Wave B · Prove that the course produces work

Order: C03 → C06 → C07 → C08 → C10

- Goal: show the Brand Kit, visual extensions, video QA and task board as inspectable outputs.
- CTA: view the complete example or inspect the course modules.
- Each asset proves one thing. Do not squeeze every deliverable into one graphic.

### Wave C · Explain fit and convert

Order: C09 → C11 → C12

- Goal: connect the educational material to the eight modules and answer “Is this for me?”
- Before commercial approval, the CTA is limited to viewing the curriculum or registering interest.
- Dates, price, place and registration link can appear only after approval and end-to-end testing.

## 7. Four ready-to-use copy briefs

### P01 · Content system

**Hook:** You do not need more content ideas. You need to stop starting from zero.

1. Daily topic hunting keeps a small team trapped by the next deadline.
2. Turn customer questions into five stable content pillars.
3. Give every idea evidence, a format, CTA, required asset and owner before production.
4. AI can then work inside one system instead of creating a larger pile of disconnected drafts.

**CTA:** View the eight-module roadmap and find the missing part of your current workflow.

### P02 · Brand consistency

**Hook:** The biggest AI design problem is not quality. It is brand drift.

Show the same business without and with a Brand Source of Truth. Compare logo use, colour, typography, image direction, CTA treatment and prohibited elements.

**CTA:** Put your last three campaign graphics side by side. Do they look like the same business?

### P03 · Platform-native production

**Hook:** Copying one post to nine platforms is not automation.

Facts and evidence can be reused. The hook, reading rhythm, dimensions, CTA and interaction pattern need to be rebuilt for each platform.

**CTA:** Take one existing post and rewrite it as a LinkedIn opener, an Instagram carousel and a short-video hook.

### P04 · AI business delivery

**Hook:** AI made the draft. Who owns review, when is it due and what is blocking delivery?

Use a Project Brief, Company Task Board, meeting actions and Weekly Review to move work from draft to Done.

**CTA:** Add an Owner, Deadline, Definition of Done and next action to one stalled task.

## 10. Measurement plan

| Funnel stage | Record | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| Attention | Reach, three-second video views, meaningful dwell | Did the hook stop the intended audience? |
| Value | Saves, carousel completion, 50% video views | Was the material worth keeping? |
| Intent | Course-page visits, roadmap/curriculum views, enquiry clicks | Which topic produced genuine interest? |
| Lead | Contactable enquiry with consent for follow-up | Channel quality, not raw volume |
| Qualified | Real business use case, course fit and ability to attend | Persona and offer fit |
| Enrolment | Successful payment with confirmation | Record only after the enrolment flow is live |
| Delivery | Attendance, module completion and D+7 review | Did delivery match the campaign promise? |

Do not invent conversion targets without a historical baseline. Compare each platform only with its own genuinely comparable past content, then record Stop / Change / Continue / Scale.

Use `utm_campaign=ai-marketing-agile-business-{intake}`. Set `utm_source` to the real platform and `utm_medium` to `organic-social`, `community`, `email`, `webinar` or `referral`. Keep `{intake}` as a placeholder until the intake is confirmed.

## 11. Production and launch gates

### Before evergreen assets enter production

- Every factual claim traces to the curriculum or an authorised case.
- Images and videos pass dimensions, rights, spelling and mobile QA.
- Every platform has an independent variant; a candidate is never labelled approved.

### Before an enrolment CTA can publish

- Programme dates, format, location, price, offers, capacity and refund terms are approved.
- Registration, payment, invoice, confirmation email and pre-course intake pass end-to-end testing.
- Instructor, support, online meeting or venue is locked.
- Account, language, media, human approval and duplicate checks all pass.

## 12. Recommended execution order

1. Select one authorised, publicly usable small business as the campaign proof business.
2. Produce the five visible demos first: C03, C04, C05, C08 and C10.
3. Assemble C01–C12 from the same evidence set instead of creating conflicting versions of the facts.
4. Confirm dates, price, capacity, instructor and enrolment flow, then update C12 and the conversion CTA.
5. Only after an explicit publishing request, enter `new-media-manager`: create the SOURCE, platform variants, media manifest, human approval, Metricool duplicate check and provider read-back.
