## 2. Required fields for each candidate

Each candidate requires all of the following, with a traceable source attached to every field:

1. A specific one-line profile: role, location, business context and triggering event.
2. Three pain points quoted in the customer's own words.
3. A measured buying cycle and the events between discovery and purchase.
4. Top five active platforms, times and reasons for use.
5. Three trusted sources and why they are trusted.
6. Three to five messages or visual patterns that create distrust.
7. Two to three concrete purchase triggers.
8. Three objections in the customer's own words and the required product response.
9. Channel ROI ranked from actual lead-source and enrolment data.
10. A source register linking interviews, de-identified conversations and records.

All ten fields for Candidates A, B and C remain `[source: pending]`.

## 4. People this course is not designed for

These are product boundaries from the course design, not research findings:

1. People without a real product, service, event, store or personal brand to work on.
2. People collecting prompts without intending to produce and review real business assets.
3. People expecting unattended customer acquisition or guaranteed growth.
4. People seeking a paid-media, CRM, sales-management or complete ecommerce programme.
