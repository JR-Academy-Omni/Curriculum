课堂工作成果

三项任务，完整交付

先阅读完整输出，再对照输入材料、指令和复核依据。三份内容承接同一组课堂工作材料。

Customer reply email

Turn meeting decisions into a clear reply without inventing commitments.

Subject: Updated onboarding guide Hello, We have noted your request to receive the updated guide by Friday. The team will prepare the draft and check its links and forms before confirming delivery arrangements. Friday delivery has not yet been confirmed. We will confirm an available delivery date after internal review. Please let us know if any onboarding steps should take priority so the team can plan the content. Thank you.
查看材料、指令与核查方法

Input

Classroom task | Monday service meeting: a customer wants the new onboarding guide by Friday. Maya will draft it by Tuesday close of business. Leo will check links and forms by Wednesday noon. The department lead has not confirmed a delivery date.

Prompt

Write a customer email using only the meeting material. State the request, agreed tasks and unconfirmed delivery date. Do not invent commitments, approvals or progress. Return a subject and body, then check every factual statement against the input.

Check

The request is preserved without becoming a commitment. Drafting and review are not described as already completed.

Meeting action list

Keep owners, deadlines and open questions separate.

1. Draft guide | Maya | Tuesday close of business | To do. 2. Check links and forms | Leo | Wednesday noon | To do. 3. Confirm customer delivery date | Department lead | Deadline to confirm | Open. Human follow-up: • When can the department lead confirm delivery? • Does Leo need Maya’s draft before starting? The meeting did not establish this dependency.
查看材料、指令与核查方法

Input

Classroom task | Maya owns the guide draft, due Tuesday close of business. Leo owns the links and forms check, due Wednesday noon. The department lead must confirm delivery, but no deadline for that confirmation was agreed.

Prompt

Extract tasks, owners, deadlines and open questions. Use only stated facts; mark missing information for confirmation. Separate agreed decisions from customer requests. Do not infer that tasks are complete.

Check

Both stated deadlines are preserved. No deadline is invented for the lead. An unstated dependency remains a question.

One-page workflow brief

Make a practical recommendation grounded in the supplied material.

Topic: Improving onboarding-guide communication Recommendation: Trial a four-step process: meeting notes → action list → human verification → customer reply draft. Keep lead approval. Evidence: Source A contains two agreed deadlines and an unconfirmed delivery date. Source B describes manual action extraction and lead approval. Process: Extract tasks with a reusable prompt; flag missing dates and dependencies; draft the reply after clarification; ask the lead to check facts and commitments before sending. Controls: Use approved tools and material; do not send automatically; compare the draft against the original notes. Trial: For the next comparable meeting, record preparation and review time plus omissions and errors, then compare against the existing process. The lead still needs to assign trial owners and dates. Limit: Without baseline data, no savings percentage or return on investment can be claimed.
查看材料、指令与核查方法

Input

Classroom source A: the meeting above. Classroom source B: staff currently prepare action lists manually, then obtain lead approval for customer replies. No time or error-rate baseline exists.

Prompt

Using sources A and B, write a one-page brief with a recommendation, evidence, four steps, controls and a trial method. Separate facts from proposals. Do not calculate savings without data. Identify open decisions.

Check

Facts trace to A or B. The workflow is a proposal, not a completed execution. No performance figures or customer outcomes are invented.
