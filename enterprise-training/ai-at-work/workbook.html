Sources A & B

CLASSROOM TASK MATERIAL — SERVICE TEAM MEETING Source A: Monday meeting notes The customer requests the new onboarding guide by Friday. Maya owns the draft, due Tuesday close of business. Leo owns the links and forms check, due Wednesday noon. The department lead must confirm customer delivery; no deadline for that confirmation was agreed. The meeting did not establish whether Leo needs the draft before starting. Source B: Current team process Staff manually prepare action lists after meetings. The department lead approves customer replies. No preparation-time or error-rate baseline has been recorded.

Lab 1 · Customer reply

Read Source A. Highlight the customer request and agreed internal tasks. Draft the reply without promising an unconfirmed date. Compare every factual statement against the material. Revise once after peer feedback.

Reusable instruction

Write a customer email using only the meeting material. State the request, agreed tasks and unconfirmed delivery date. Do not invent commitments, approvals or progress. Return a subject and body, then check every factual statement against the input.

Lab 2 · Action list

Extract tasks, owners, deadlines and status. Add an open-questions section. Check that no unknown deadline or dependency became a fact. Revision update: Leo can start only after Maya shares the draft. Revise the dependency while keeping all deadlines unchanged.

Reusable instruction

Extract tasks, owners, deadlines and open questions. Use only stated facts; mark missing information for confirmation. Separate agreed decisions from customer requests. Do not infer that tasks are complete.

Lab 3 · One-page brief

Use Sources A and B. State a recommendation, then cite its evidence. Describe controls and a small trial. Explicitly state why no time-saving percentage can be calculated. Revise after a peer labels each sentence as fact, recommendation or open decision.

Reusable instruction

Using sources A and B, write a one-page brief with a recommendation, evidence, four steps, controls and a trial method. Separate facts from proposals. Do not calculate savings without data. Identify open decisions.

Lab 4 · Workflow opportunity card

Choose one low-risk task. Draw four steps. Mark a human review gate before external use. Define what happens when information is missing. Decide what baseline to measure and who should approve a trial.

Reusable instruction

Create a four-step workflow card for one approved team task. Include input, output, reviewer, failure route and task owner. Do not enable automatic sending or production changes.

Independent transfer task

INDEPENDENT TRANSFER TASK Use only these handover notes. Prepare a customer email and an action list. Separate confirmed facts from open decisions. A customer requests an attendance summary by Thursday. Priya will check the attendance file by Tuesday 3 pm. Ben will prepare the summary after the file check, but no draft deadline has been agreed. The team lead must approve the summary before sending. The team lead has not confirmed Thursday delivery. Do not send anything. Identify missing information and the explicit dependency. Submit your outputs and a short review checklist.
