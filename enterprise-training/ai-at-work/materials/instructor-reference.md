## Review criteria
All stated tasks are included; the explicit dependency is preserved. No draft deadline or delivery promise is invented. Lead approval is retained before sending. Equivalent wording is acceptable.

## Workflow opportunity card reference
Task: prepare a meeting action list and customer reply draft.
Input: approved meeting notes and confirmed customer requirements.
Step 1: collect approved material and flag missing information.
Step 2: extract an action list and prepare a reply draft.
Step 3: a named reviewer checks facts, deadlines and commitments against the source; return incomplete work to step 1.
Step 4: the department lead approves external use; sending remains a separate human action.
Output: reviewed action list and approved reply text.
Open decisions: the company must choose the actual reviewer, trial owner, start date and approved tool.
Baseline: record existing preparation and review time, omissions, unsupported claims and rework before comparing any trial.
