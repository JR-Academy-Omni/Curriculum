# Milk Supply Agreement

**Supplier:** Riverbend Dairy Co Pty Ltd  
**Customer:** Morning Light Cafe Group Pty Ltd (3 cafes: Fortitude Valley, New Farm, West End)  
**Reference:** RBD-SA-9926  
**Supplier contact:** accounts@riverbend.example

---

## 1. Term

This Agreement starts on 1 December 2026 and continues for 24 months. It expires at the end of that period unless both parties agree in writing to extend it.

## 2. Products and Price

Full cream and skim milk in 10 L bags at AUD 1.68 per litre (exclusive of GST, if any). Prices are fixed for the first 12 months. After that, the Supplier may review prices once every 12 months by no more than the change in the Brisbane CPI, with 30 days' written notice.

## 3. Ordering and Delivery

Orders by 2 pm for next-morning delivery, Monday to Saturday. Delivery is included in the price. Minimum order: none.

## 4. Payment

Invoices are payable 14 days after the end of the month of delivery.

## 5. Quality and Returns

Product must be delivered at or below 5°C with at least 7 days' shelf life. The Customer may reject non-conforming product at delivery and receive a credit.

## 6. Liability

Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees paid or payable by the Customer in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim. This limit does not apply to personal injury or to liability that cannot be excluded by law.

## 7. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's pricing and business information confidential.

## 8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Queensland.
