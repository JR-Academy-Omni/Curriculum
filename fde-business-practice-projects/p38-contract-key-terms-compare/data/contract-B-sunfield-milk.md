## 1. Term

The Initial Term is 12 months from 1 December 2026.

## 2. Renewal

At the end of the Initial Term and each Renewal Term, this Agreement automatically renews for a further 12 months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Products and Price

Full cream and skim milk in 10 L bags at AUD 1.59 per litre (exclusive of GST, if any). The Supplier may change prices by giving 30 days' written notice.

## 4. Ordering and Delivery

Orders by 12 noon for next-day delivery. A delivery fee of AUD 12.00 applies to each drop. Minimum order: none.

## 5. Payment

Invoices are payable within 7 days of the invoice date.

## 6. Quality and Returns

Product is delivered chilled. Claims for damaged or warm product must be made within 24 hours of delivery with photos.

## 7. Liability

The Supplier's total liability is limited to the fees paid by the Customer in the 12 months before the claim.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate if the other commits a material breach and does not fix it within 14 days of written notice. After the Initial Term, either party may terminate for convenience with 60 days' written notice.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Queensland.
