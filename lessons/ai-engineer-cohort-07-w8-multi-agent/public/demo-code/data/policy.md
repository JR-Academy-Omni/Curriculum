# Banksia Wing documentation policy (synthetic, for training only)

This text is invented for class. It is not legal or clinical guidance and is not the Aged Care Quality Standards or the SIRS rules.

## P-DOC-1 Progress notes
A progress note records what staff observed and did. Use only confirmed observations. Do not include unconfirmed voice transcripts. Do not diagnose, and do not state causes that were not observed. Every AI-written note is a Draft until a staff member with the right role reviews and confirms it.

## P-FALL-2 Falls
After any fall or found-on-floor event, the note must state: time found, what the resident said, injury check result, who assessed, post-fall observations started, and family contact. Possible head strike requires the RN to decide on neuro observations.

## P-NUT-3 Nutrition and declined meals
Two or more declined main meals in one day must be noted and passed to the RN at handover for weight and nutrition review.

## P-MED-4 Medication refusal
Record the medication round, the refusal, any re-offer and its result, and that the RN was informed. Time-critical medications delayed more than 30 minutes go to the RN at handover.

## P-INC-5 Possible reportable incidents
An unexplained injury, or a resident saying someone hurt them, is a possible reportable incident. Staff must inform the RN immediately. Whether it is reportable, and to whom, is decided only by the RN and the facility manager. AI must never decide or state that an incident is or is not reportable; it may only flag it for RN review.
