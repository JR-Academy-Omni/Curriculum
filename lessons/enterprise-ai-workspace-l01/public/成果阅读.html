# 企业介绍

Harbour Build Studio supports small commercial property owners and business operators with commercial interior refurbishment coordination. Its services cover scope organisation, quotation preparation, procurement coordination and project documentation. These services help organise the information needed for project discussions and follow-up work. The four-person team covers project leadership, estimating coordination, site coordination, and administration and finance coordination. Client communication uses clear, direct English, with the scope of work confirmed before further discussions are arranged. For an initial discussion, share the proposed work and the information already available. Pricing, terms and other details that have not been provided remain subject to confirmation, so the introduction makes no commitments beyond the available company information.

## 依据与审核
C01 支持受众；C02 支持服务；C03 支持团队职责；C04 支持语气与沟通安排。未加入资质、历史、客户证明、价格或联系信息。正式对外使用前由负责人核对企业实体信息与服务描述。
