## Task 6 — Diagnose comparable data

Use `practice/measurement-scenario.csv`. It contains two equal fourteen-day synthetic windows. It assumes consistent event definitions and cohort selection within the exercise. It is not an export from Search Console or Analytics.

Calculate search CTR, checklist-completion share within the P2 session cohort, and P1-entry share within the same cohort. Do not add overlapping behaviors as separate people. Do not equate clicks with sessions. Explain why missing leads cannot be entered as zero.

**Worked calculations:**

| Metric | Window A | Window B |
|---|---|---|
| Search CTR | 60 / 1000 = 6% | 72 / 1200 = 6% |
| Checklist completion share | 12 / 58 = 20.69% | 24 / 70 = 34.29% |
| P1 entry share | 6 / 58 = 10.34% | 12 / 70 = 17.14% |

**Worked diagnostic memo:** Within this synthetic scenario, checklist completion and P1 entry shares increase while search CTR stays constant. The data do not establish a causal effect or commercial demand. Review event consistency and visitor composition, then obtain authorized evidence about whether the method helps. Lead and payment outcomes remain unmeasured or not applicable because the required business paths do not exist.

Submit three prioritized actions. Each must include the trigger evidence, user affected, change scope, owner, permission dependency, and acceptance criterion.

## Final project dossier

Submit the six outputs as one coherent dossier. Use consistent page IDs, user needs, claims, source records, and event definitions. The dossier must explain the chain from the initial user task to the next iteration.

| Review dimension | Ready | Needs revision |
|---|---|---|
| Logical continuity | Outputs consistently follow from earlier decisions | Pages, claims, or measures appear without a reason |
| Complete content | At least one full page fulfills its promise | Only an outline, title list, or promotional shell |
| Evidence integrity | Observed, simulated, expected, and missing evidence are distinct | Invented proof, authors, reviews, or results |
| Product honesty | Available actions and limitations are explicit | A case page is presented as a working product demo |
| Measurement reasoning | Ratios use compatible sources and scopes | Impressions, clicks, sessions, and sales are conflated |
| Next action | Specific, evidence-led, and verifiable | Generic instruction to publish more or chase rankings |

This is a course review rubric, not a Google E-E-A-T score. Do not infer real-world success from a complete fictional dossier.
