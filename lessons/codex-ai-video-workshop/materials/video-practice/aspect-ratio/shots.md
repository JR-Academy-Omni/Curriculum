# Key-shot action specification

S1: A landscape frame containing a central circle and two side objects enters a portrait canvas.
S2: Uniformly scale to fit width; all objects remain, vertical space is visible.
S3: Uniformly scale to fill height; side objects move beyond the visible canvas; circle stays circular.
S4: Briefly compare non-uniform stretching; label it as distortion, not the recommended fix.
S5: Return to a readable contain/cover comparison and hold.

Every shot needs a stable ID, defined time, readable result and transition anchor. No wall-clock timers or unseeded randomness. For a 12-second preview, implement a subset with its own correct timing instead of claiming all provisional full-film shots fit.
