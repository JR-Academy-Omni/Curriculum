# Fictional Event Services Agreement

Training text only; not a signed agreement.

## 1. Parties
Cedar Events (Customer) and Lumen Event Studio (Supplier), both fictional.

## 2. Services
Supplier will provide four hours of photography at the Customer event on 2026-11-14, 13:00–17:00 Brisbane local time, at Cedar Hall (fictional).

## 3. Deliverables
Supplier will provide 40 edited JPEG photographs within 10 calendar days after the event. RAW files and video are excluded. One selection revision is included; additional work requires a separately accepted quote.

## 4. Fees
Total fee AUD 1100 inclusive of the stated sample tax amount. Deposit AUD 330 after written acceptance. Balance AUD 770 within 7 calendar days of delivery. No payment details are provided.

## 5. Cancellation
Cancellation more than 7 calendar days before the event permits a deposit refund less documented third-party costs. Cancellation 7 days or fewer before the event forfeits the deposit. No cap on third-party costs is stated.

## 6. Use of images
Customer may use delivered photographs for its own event communications. Supplier may use identifiable guest images for promotion only with separately documented permission from the relevant individuals. No such permission is supplied with this agreement.

## 7. Unresolved terms
The parties have not specified governing law, dispute process, backup photographer arrangements or a limit of liability. No signature or acceptance date appears in this training text.
